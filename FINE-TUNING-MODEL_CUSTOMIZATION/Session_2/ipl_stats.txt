Mumbai Indians have won the IPL title 5 times (2013, 2015, 2017, 2019, 2020).
Chennai Super Kings have won the IPL title 5 times (2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, 2023).
Kolkata Knight Riders have won the IPL title 3 times (2012, 2014, 2024).
Royal Challengers Bengaluru won their first IPL title in 2025.
Virat Kohli is the all-time leading run-scorer in IPL history with over 8000 runs.
Chris Gayle holds the highest individual IPL score of 175 not out off 66 balls for RCB in 2013.
Sunrisers Hyderabad scored 287 for 3 against RCB in 2024, the highest team total in IPL history.
Yuzvendra Chahal is among the leading wicket-takers in IPL history with over 200 wickets.
Vaibhav Suryavanshi scored a 35-ball century for Rajasthan Royals in 2025 at the age of 14.
MS Dhoni captained Chennai Super Kings to multiple titles and is known as Captain Cool.
Jasprit Bumrah is Mumbai Indians' premier fast bowler known for his yorkers.
Gujarat Titans won the IPL title in 2022 in their debut season.
