## Task 2: Discriminative Models vs Generative Models

| Aspect                  | Discriminative Models                                                                        | Generative Models                                                     |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Definition**          | Learn the relationship between input data and output labels and distinguish between classes. | Learn patterns or the distribution of data and can generate new data. |
| **Main use**            | Classification, prediction, and decision-making.                                             | Generating new text, images, audio, video, or other content.          |
| **Example task**        | Determine whether an email is spam or not spam.                                              | Generate a new email or write a reply.                                |
| **Output**              | Usually a class, label, probability, or numerical prediction.                                | New content that resembles the patterns learned during training.      |
| **Example model**       | Logistic Regression, SVM, Decision Tree, Random Forest.                                      | GPT, GAN, VAE, Diffusion Models.                                      |
| **Example application** | Spotify predicting which songs a user may like.                                              | ChatGPT generating a personalized music playlist description.         |

Simple Explanation

A **discriminative model** mainly answers:

> "Which category does this data belong to?"

A **generative model** mainly answers:

> "Can I create new data that follows the patterns I have learned?"

**Example:**

```text
Discriminative:
Image → Model → Cat

Generative:
Text Prompt → Model → New Cat Image
```

## Task 3: Flipkart Gadget Product Description

Fictional Gadget

**Product Name:** SmartGlow DeskHub

SmartGlow DeskHub is an AI-powered smart desk accessory designed for students, programmers, and professionals. It combines a wireless charging pad, adjustable LED desk light, digital clock, and voice-controlled AI assistant in one compact device.

Prompt Used

Create a professional and attractive Flipkart product description for a fictional gadget called "SmartGlow DeskHub". It is an AI-powered smart desk accessory designed for students, programmers, and professionals. The device includes a wireless charging pad, adjustable LED desk light, digital clock, and voice-controlled AI assistant. Highlight its practical benefits, modern design, convenience, and productivity features. Use simple language suitable for Indian online shoppers. Make the description exciting but realistic and keep it under 150 words. End with a catchy one-line tagline.

ChatGPT Output

**SmartGlow DeskHub – Your Smarter Desk Companion**

Upgrade your workspace with the **SmartGlow DeskHub**, an all-in-one smart desk accessory designed for modern students, programmers, and professionals. Its sleek design combines a **wireless charging pad, adjustable LED desk light, digital clock, and voice-controlled AI assistant** in one compact device.

Charge your smartphone wirelessly while the adjustable LED light provides comfortable illumination for studying, coding, or working late at night. The built-in digital clock keeps you on schedule, while the voice-controlled AI assistant helps you manage simple tasks and reminders without interrupting your workflow.

Compact, stylish, and practical, SmartGlow DeskHub keeps your essential desk features within easy reach.

**SmartGlow DeskHub — Charge. Light. Organize. Work Smarter.**

Why the detailed prompt is useful

The prompt provides the AI with:

1. The product name.
2. The target customers.
3. The product features.
4. The desired writing style.
5. The target audience.
6. A word limit.
7. The desired ending.

Therefore, the AI can generate a more specific and useful product description.

## Task 4: New Generative AI Feature

Application Selected: Text Generation

App: Zomato

I would add a **Generative AI Food Assistant** to Zomato. Instead of searching using multiple filters, users could simply describe what they want in natural language. For example, a user could type, "I want a healthy vegetarian dinner for two people under ₹800, preferably somewhere quiet." The Generative AI model would understand the user's requirements, identify the important preferences, and use restaurant information to generate personalized recommendations. It could also explain why each restaurant is suitable for the user's request.

How Generative AI would power the feature

text
User's Natural-Language Request
            ↓
      Generative AI Model
            ↓
Understand User Intent
            ↓
Budget + Cuisine + Location + Preferences
            ↓
Restaurant Database
            ↓
Personalized AI Response


Example

**User:**

> "I want a good Italian dinner for two under ₹1,000."

**AI Assistant:**

> "Here are three Italian restaurants that fit your budget. I recommend Pizza Palace because it has several Italian options for two people within ₹900 and has good customer ratings."

Why GenAI is better here

A traditional rule-based system may require the user to manually select:

* Cuisine → Italian
* Price → Under ₹1,000
* People → 2
* Restaurant type → Dinner

With GenAI, the user can simply **describe their requirements naturally**, and the AI can understand the request and generate a personalized response.

Conclusion

Generative AI can make everyday applications more interactive and user-friendly because users do not always need to follow predefined commands or filters. It can understand natural language and generate responses or content based on the user's specific needs.
